01

The Look

Precision is the 2026 foundation. One hard key light, a near-neutral grade, and a frame stripped of anything that isn’t the vehicle. Surfaces carry the story — a single continuous specular running the length of the shoulder line, shadows that stay closed, colour that never announces itself.

It should read as engineering documentation, shot beautifully. Composed, not styled. If a frame needs a second light or a second idea to hold together, it isn’t this style — move the one light, or move the camera.

01
One light
Everything visible in the frame is explained by a single source. Reflections agree with each other.
02
Closed shadow
Black stays black. Detail that falls below the shadow line is allowed to leave.
03
Neutral ground
Built surfaces, no colour of their own. The only chroma in frame belongs to the car’s own lamps.

03

The Recipe

The eight parameters that make a frame belong to this set. Values are the specification; the line beneath each one is the reason it holds.

Light
1 hard key · 40° · ratio 1:8 · no fill card
The key sits high and to one side so the specular reads as one unbroken ribbon down the shoulder line. If the ribbon breaks, move the light — never add a second one.
Lens
85 / 135 mm prime · 100 mm macro · never < 50 mm
Long glass compresses the body into the shape the designers drew. Anything wider bends the sills and reads immediately as consumer photography.
Aperture & focus
f/8–f/11 on form · f/2.8 on detail
Focus lands on the leading edge of the nearest panel, never on a badge. The whole body stays sharp; only macro work is allowed to fall off.
Grade
neutral · sat −18 · black point 0 · warm hi / cool lo
A split tone, not a colour cast: highlights warm a little, shadows cool a little, and the midtones stay where they were. Never lift the blacks to show detail.
Composition
1 dominant diagonal · subject 62% of frame width
One line does the work — the shoulder, the roofline or the road. Nothing crosses behind the roof; the horizon is suppressed or cropped out entirely.
Environment
concrete · asphalt · seamless — built surfaces only
Nothing organic, nothing weather-driven, no people. The location is a plane and a wall; if it has a personality of its own, it is the wrong location.
Motion
1/200 s locked · 1/8 s pan for road
The body is always sharp. Movement is carried by the ground and the background, and any light trail belongs to the car’s own lamps — never to traffic.
Surface & finish
satin · no clear-coat glare · grain retained
Paint keeps its micro-grain and carbon weave stays readable at 100%. A blown, glassy highlight is a mistake, not a flourish.
04

What Holds It, What Breaks It

Both frames below are real Corvache photography. Only one of them is Precision — which is the whole point: the difference is a set of decisions, not a subject.

This holds

Black on black, one hard highlight, no set colour anywhere. The material is the subject.

  • Let the shadow close — detail below the shadow line is allowed to leave.
  • Keep one light source legible in every reflection in frame.
  • Crop in until the material, not the object, is the subject.
  • Let neutral surfaces carry the colour story.
This breaks it

A saturated set competing with the paint, diffuse light from everywhere, no single source to read.

  • Don’t introduce a set colour that competes with the body.
  • Don’t add a second key to rescue a shape the first one missed.
  • Don’t lift the blacks to “show detail” in the shadow.
  • Don’t place the car small inside a landscape.

Prompt Fragment

The recipe above, written the way a model reads it. Paste it ahead of your own subject line — it carries the style, you bring the shot.

Style — 2026 Corvache Precision 
Shot in the 2026 Corvache Precision style: a single hard key light at 40 degrees, no fill, lighting ratio 1:8, one continuous specular ribbon running the length of the shoulder line, shadows fully closed with the black point held at zero. Captured on an 85mm prime at f/8, 1/200s, ISO 100 — long glass, no wide-angle distortion. Neutral grade, desaturated body colour, warm highlights against cool shadows, no global colour cast. Composed on one dominant diagonal with the subject filling roughly 62% of the frame width and the horizon suppressed. Built environment only: concrete, asphalt or seamless studio floor. Satin finish, no clear-coat glare, visible micro-grain in the paint. 3:2 frame.
Avoid 
wide-angle distortion, second key light, coloured gels, lens flare, motion blur on the body, foliage, crowds, weather effects, glossy clear-coat glare, lifted blacks, saturated set colour, square crop
