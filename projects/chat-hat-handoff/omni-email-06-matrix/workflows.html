Introducing Workflows.

Workflows let you chain agents into something bigger than a single prompt. Give each step its own model, agent, knowledge base and tools, then wire the steps together and run the whole thing end to end.
Try / Catch branches on failure — each path lands in its own typed output.
Logic blocks decide what happens next — branch on a guardrail, retry on failure, or stop and hand off to a person. Output blocks pin down exactly what comes out the other end, in the shape you asked for.
